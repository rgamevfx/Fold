Shared Camera Plane mapping preparation — 8 October 2026
M4 preparation only; native placement and complete M3–M5 gates remain open.

fold-render::spatial::Camera::plane resolves logical top-left/Y-down canvas points
to a plane attached to an immutable camera snapshot. Distance is positive camera-
axis meters. Viewport sizing spans the full raster, with explicit per-axis scale
when canvas/raster aspect ratios differ. MetersPerUnit preserves physical size.
Perspective and orthographic projections share the same basis and validation.
Mapping uses f64 arithmetic until the renderer-owned f32 position boundary;
nonfinite/out-of-range points fail. Normal faces the camera; Y-down triangles
reverse winding. Existing spatial clipping and depth apply without a new renderer.
Scene and mapping now reuse one camera validator, preserving existing validation.

Verification:

  off-axis and rolled bases, both projections, two distances and both size policies;
  top-left/center/bottom-right positions and facing normals match analytic values.
