Native Camera Plane spatial preparation — 8 October 2026
CPU preparation evidence; native GPU appearance remains unverified.

Camera Plane now joins adjacent World entries in one spatial image pass; Screen
entries retain ordered compositing and separate spatial depth runs. Conversion
uses the explicit evaluated attachment camera, shared Camera::plane mapping and
shared planar path tessellator. Text and Outline Text reuse Screen's retained path
extraction, preserving source/layout provenance. Fill and stroke produce separate
unlit meshes/materials; placement opacity multiplies paint alpha once. Projected
scale accounts for viewport/physical sizing, projection, distance and glyph affine
transform. Geometry resource IDs derive from occurrence/path/paint-part identity.

Mapped mesh positions cross the f32 renderer boundary before final facing selection.
Tiny glyph triangles can collapse or invert there. Preparation removes collapsed
triangles and selects camera-facing winding from final coordinates, including
Y-up glyph transforms and Y-down canvas paths. This fixed a failure in the new
native text preparation test; no tolerance was relaxed.

Current limitation: the attached camera must be the active scene camera, with
matching identity/content. A different view camera explicitly errors. General
cross-camera projected subdivision and clipping still require implementation.
